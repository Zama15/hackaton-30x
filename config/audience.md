# Audience profile — 30X

The audience-fit scorer reads this file. **The media team should edit it**; it is the main lever for relevance.

## Who we make content for
- Leaders, executives and managers in Latin America (MX, CO, AR, CL, PE and the rest of LatAm) who are learning to use AI in their business and teams.
- Teams inside companies adopting AI: sales, marketing, operations, HR, finance.
- They're mostly not engineers. They want practical, applicable value, not research papers.

## Language and tone
- Spanish (LatAm neutral) first. English sources are fine as inspiration, but the output must be adapted to Spanish.
- Direct, practical, optimistic but not hype. Concrete examples from LatAm companies are a plus.

## Strong fit signals
- Helps a leader decide, adopt, or lead a team through AI change.
- Productivity, strategy, careers and future of work, AI tools for business, AI agents applied to business processes.
- Can be explained in < 60 s or a 7-slide carousel.

## Weak or no fit
- Deep technical/ML research content without a business angle.
- Crypto, get-rich-quick, or clickbait "AI will replace you" fear content without substance.
- Content that only makes sense for the US market.
